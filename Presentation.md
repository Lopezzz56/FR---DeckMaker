# FocusRealm Deck Maker — Presentation Redesign

Create a new `presentation.md` that defines how the Deck Maker should generate professional, effective presentations for FocusRealm and its product, Mise Hotel.

## 1. Presentation structure

Do not restrict presentations to exactly eight slides. Generate the number of slides needed to explain the story clearly without unnecessary repetition.

**Section A — FocusRealm introduction**
Introduce FocusRealm, the hospitality industry's operational problems, Mise Hotel's solution, its core modules, and the practical value it provides.

**Section B — Personalized company presentation**
Research the target hotel or hospitality company, understand its business and brand, identify relevant opportunities, explain how Mise Hotel addresses them, and propose a practical next step.

Keep the FocusRealm introduction consistent across presentations. Personalize the company-specific section using research and verified information.

## 2. Learn from the Mathur Hospitality reference

Study the attached Mathur Hospitality PDF to understand what makes its product presentation effective.

In particular, learn from its:
- Large, clearly visible product screenshots.
- Dedicated slides explaining the staff's mobile experience and the manager's dashboard.
- Clear headlines that communicate one specific point.
- Screenshots paired with concise explanations of what the user can do.
- Straightforward layouts that explain the product rather than decorate the slide.

Apply these principles to the existing FocusRealm presentation. Do not copy the reference's branding or visual theme.

## 3. Fix the existing presentation problems

The current presentation is a good starting point, but it needs improvement:

- Leadership and advisor portraits are excessively cropped, making faces difficult to recognize.
- Product screenshots are too small to communicate Mise Hotel's actual functionality.
- The staff mobile interface and management dashboard need greater prominence.
- Some slides contain enormous empty spaces and poorly distributed content.
- Several slides have headings and cards separated by excessive whitespace.
- The slides feel like website layouts adapted to PowerPoint instead of presentations designed for a full-screen, 16:9 display.

Redesign the slide layouts for widescreen presentations. Use appropriate image proportions, larger visuals, balanced spacing and readable text.

## 4. Explain Mise Hotel clearly

Give sufficient attention to the actual product modules and workflows.

Show the product in use through genuine screenshots, with clear explanations of:
- What the staff member sees and does.
- How managers assign work, supervise execution and handle exceptions.
- How standards, task completion and supporting evidence are recorded.

Use separate slides for different modules or user experiences when necessary. Do not squeeze everything into one slide.

Every product slide should answer: **What am I looking at, what does it help someone do, and why does it matter?**

## 5. Presentation design principles

Keep the design simple, professional and consistent with FocusRealm's existing brand.

- One clear message per slide.
- Strong, meaningful headlines.
- Large product screenshots or relevant photographs when they help explain the point.
- Short supporting text instead of paragraphs.
- Whitespace that improves readability, not empty space that wastes the slide.
- Consistent typography, alignment, margins and image treatment.
- Simple diagrams only when they make a workflow easier to understand.

Avoid unnecessary animations, decorative graphics, generic AI-generated imagery, excessive icons, complicated diagrams and layouts that prioritize appearance over communication.

**A simple slide that communicates the point clearly is better than an elaborate slide that communicates nothing.**

## 6. Research and personalization

The user supplies a hotel/company name and optionally its website, documents or notes.

Research the company and personalize the relevant slides using its real brand identity, properties, positioning and credible business information.

Identify plausible opportunities for Mise Hotel, clearly distinguishing verified facts from hypotheses. Connect each opportunity to relevant product capabilities and propose a realistic next step.

Never invent company facts, product capabilities, customer relationships, statistics or results. Preserve source URLs for researched claims.

## 7. Update the existing Deck Maker

Create `presentation.md` as the central reference for presentation structure, content, design principles and quality standards.

Inspect the existing application and integrate these guidelines into its actual generation workflow. Do not merely create a markdown file and leave the application unchanged.

Preserve the existing FocusRealm branding, approved company content, product assets and working functionality. Improve the slide-generation logic so the website preview, PowerPoint and PDF exports remain visually consistent.

Before considering the work complete, inspect the generated slides at full-screen presentation size and verify that images, spacing, typography and messaging work properly.

**Final objective:** A clear, credible sales presentation that helps a hotel understand FocusRealm, see Mise Hotel in action, recognize its relevance to their operations and understand the next step.
